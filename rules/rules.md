Chinese Checkers is a **race across the board!** Your goal is to move all of your marbles from your starting triangle to the triangle on the **opposite side of the star** before anyone else does.

---

## 🎯 The Goal

Each player has **10 marbles of one color**.

Your marbles begin together in one of the six points of the star. The triangle directly across from you is your **goal triangle**.

> 🏆 **Be the first player to get all 10 of your marbles into your goal triangle to win!**

---

## 🎲 Setting Up the Game

Chinese Checkers can be played with **2, 3, 4, or 6 players**. Each player chooses a color and places all **10 marbles** in their starting triangle.

Arrange the starting triangles like this:

PlayersStarting Positions**2 Players**Start directly across from each other**3 Players**Use every other point of the star**4 Players**Use two pairs of opposite points**6 Players**Use all six points

Choose who goes first. After that, players take turns around the table.

---

# 🔵 Taking Your Turn

On your turn, choose **ONE of your marbles** to move.

You have two choices:

### 1️⃣ Move One Space

Move your marble into an **empty hole right next to it**.

```
```

```
●  ○

     ↓

○  ●
```

**●** = Your marble\
\
**○** = Empty hole

You can move in **any direction** as long as the empty hole is directly beside your marble. 

### 2️⃣ Jump Over a Marble

You can jump over a marble that is **right next to yours** if there is an empty hole directly behind it.

```
```

```
●  🔴  ○
 \_____/
   JUMP!
```

The three spaces must form a **straight line**.

You may jump over:

- \
  Your own marbles
- \
  Another player's marbles

The marble you jump over **stays exactly where it is**. There is **no capturing** in Chinese Checkers! 

---

# 🐇 Chain Jumps

Here's where Chinese Checkers gets really fun!

After making a jump, you can keep jumping with the **same marble** if another legal jump is available.

```
```

```
       ②          ③
      ↗          ↘
● → 🔴 → ○ → 🟡 → ○
①
```

You might be able to jump your marble several times and travel a huge distance in **one turn**.

For every jump:

> **Jump over exactly one nearby marble and land in the empty hole directly behind it.**

You can change direction **between jumps**, but each individual jump must follow a straight line. 

---

## ⚠️ Steps and Jumps Don't Mix

You must choose what kind of move you're making.

You can either:

**Move one space**

**OR**

**Make one or more jumps**

You **cannot move one space and then start jumping** during the same turn. 

---

# 🚫 Marbles Are Never Captured

Chinese Checkers may have **"Checkers"** in its name, but you don't capture pieces!

When you jump over another marble:

```
```

```
BEFORE

🔵  🔴  ○


AFTER

○   🔴  🔵
```

Notice that 🔴 is still there.

In fact, other players' marbles can be very helpful. You can use them as **stepping stones** to make long jumps across the board. 

---

# 🧠 Build a Path

Try to spread your marbles out so they help each other.

Instead of moving every marble one space at a time, you can create a path that lets another marble make several jumps:

```
```

```
🔵 → 🔵 → 🔴 → 🟡 → 🟢
     JUMP!  JUMP!  JUMP!
```

Remember: **you can jump over any color.**

Sometimes an opponent's marble gives you exactly the jump you need!

---

# 🏆 Winning the Game

The first player to move **all 10 of their marbles** into the 10 holes of their opposite triangle wins! 

That means getting one marble across isn't enough.

You need to bring the **whole team home!**

## 💡 Remember

Chinese Checkers isn't about knocking other players out of the game. It's about finding the **fastest path across the board**.

Watch for useful jumps, build paths with your marbles, and don't be afraid to use your opponents' marbles to help you travel farther.
